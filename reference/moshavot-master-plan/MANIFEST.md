# Moshavot Master Plan (תוכנית האב למושבות) - reference documents

Source: Ginot HaIr community council public site,
<https://www.ginothair.org.il/index.php?cs=595&dir=site&langpage=heb&page=content>
(files under <https://www.ginothair.org.il/files/wordocs/>).
All files retrieved 2026-10-19. These are the council's public documents;
the council states the approved plan documents were signed March 2015.
Files over ~10 MB are stored via Git LFS.

Per the plan's own הוראות (hanchayot.pdf, section 1.2), the plan covers the
four historic neighborhoods: German Colony (zone A), Greek Colony (zone B),
Old Katamon (zone C) and Talbiya (zone D), plus the Bruria compound, Givat
HaNasi, Gan HaPaamon and Gan San Simon. Rehavia is NOT in the plan area
(north of the plan's stated northern boundary: Jabotinsky / HaNasi /
HaPalmach streets) and is not mentioned anywhere in the documents.
